# Opening Sections Color Refresh

## Goal

Adapt the Hero, Fahrzeugverkauf, and six-step process sections to the established IMD comparison-section design language without changing their structure, copy, imagery, forms, or behavior.

## Scope

The refresh covers:

1. Homepage Hero (`#top`)
2. Fahrzeugverkauf service area (`#service`)
3. Six-step sales process (`#prozess`)

No elements are added, removed, reordered, renamed, or functionally changed.

## Shared Visual Language

Use the comparison section as the source of truth:

- Concrete base: `#CAC9C4`
- Concrete highlight: `#E5E4DF`
- Concrete medium: `#B3B4B0`
- Navy: `#202A3B`
- Graphite: `#1C2228`
- Body text: `#4C5257`
- Muted text: `#777A78`
- Border: `#9A9C99`
- Blue accent: `#36A2C5`

Use blue only for active, selected, or especially important elements. Primary actions use navy. Secondary actions use graphite or navy outlines.

## Hero

- Preserve the existing photograph and composition.
- Neutralize the left overlay from blue-black to translucent graphite.
- Keep the main heading white over the photograph and use the existing blue accent only on the emphasized line.
- Restyle the live calculator as a light concrete glass panel with navy/graphite text, subtle borders, and a navy submit button.
- Restyle the bottom benefit strip with concrete/navy surfaces and reduced visual weight.
- Preserve all form fields, labels, options, calculations, and CTA behavior.

## Fahrzeugverkauf

- Preserve the existing background image and all card positions.
- Replace near-black cards with light concrete translucent surfaces.
- Use graphite headings, body text, and navy icon treatments.
- Use navy for major section anchors and blue only for focused or highlighted states.
- Reduce heavy glow and dark glass effects in favor of subtle concrete shadows and thin gray borders.
- Preserve every card, chip, process item, CTA, and responsive layout.

## Six-Step Process

- Preserve the timeline, six step cards, side introduction panel, and bottom benefit cards.
- Replace dark gray panels with concrete medium/light surfaces.
- Use graphite text and navy step markers.
- Keep one blue accent for the active step only.
- Reduce glow, strong transparency, and bright white borders.
- Preserve active-step logic, animations, icons, and mobile behavior.

## Responsive Behavior

- Apply the same palette consistently on desktop and mobile.
- Do not alter breakpoints, grid definitions, ordering, dimensions, or interaction logic.
- Maintain readable contrast over photographic backgrounds.

## Validation

- Assert the shared palette variables are scoped to all three sections.
- Assert the Hero calculator uses a concrete surface and navy primary action.
- Assert service cards use light concrete surfaces with graphite text.
- Assert process cards use concrete surfaces and retain a blue active state.
- Confirm existing HTML structure and JavaScript remain unchanged.
- Check desktop and mobile screenshots for contrast and consistency.
